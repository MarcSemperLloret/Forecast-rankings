Four-panel figure showing that model-family roughness does not provide the predicted explanation of reference advantage. Panels a and b plot native 50-km roughness against ERA5-minus-station MAE and RMSE for IFS, GraphCast, Pangu and FuXi, with GenCast ensemble mean shown separately as a hollow diamond. The deterministic association is negative. Panel c shows negative bootstrap slopes with confidence intervals below zero at 25, 50 and 100 km. Panel d shows the common 1.5-degree result changing from positive at 300 km to uncertain at 600 km and negative at 1,200 km. The result separates causal within-forecast support effects from cross-family associations.
